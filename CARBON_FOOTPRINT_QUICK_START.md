# 🌍 Enhanced Carbon Footprint Calculator - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome! 👋

Your personal AI-powered carbon tracking dashboard is ready. Let's get you started in 3 easy steps!

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Add Your First Entry
1. Click the **"Start Tracking"** button on the hero section
2. Choose **"Quick Entry"** for fast input or **"Detailed Entry"** for precise calculations
3. Fill in your daily activities:
   - 🚗 **Transport:** Car miles, public transit, flights
   - ⚡ **Energy:** Electricity usage, gas, renewable %
   - 🍽️ **Food:** Diet type, local food percentage
   - 🛍️ **Shopping:** New purchases today
   - 🗑️ **Waste:** Recycling and composting
4. Click **"Save Entry"**

### Step 2: Set Your First Goal
1. Click **"New Goal"** button in the Goals section
2. Name your goal (e.g., "Reduce 20% in 30 days")
3. Set your reduction target (10%, 20%, 50%...)
4. Choose duration (1 week to 1 year)
5. Select focus categories
6. Click **"Create Goal"**

### Step 3: Get AI Recommendations
1. Scroll to **"AI-Powered Recommendations"**
2. View personalized suggestions based on your data
3. Click **"Commit to This"** on actions you'll take
4. See estimated CO₂ savings and money saved!

- **🎯 Goal Progress:** % towards your reduction goal

### Charts
- **Trend Chart:** Line graph showing daily emissions over time
- **Category Chart:** Pie chart of your emission sources

### 2. Goal Setting

**What makes a good goal?**
- ✅ Specific: "Reduce 15% in 60 days"
- ✅ Achievable: Start with 10-20%
- ✅ Focused: Pick 2-3 categories
- ✅ Time-bound: 30-90 days works best

**Example Goals:**
- "Cut transport emissions 30% by biking more"
- "Reduce food waste 25% in 1 month"
- "Lower energy use 20% with smart thermostat"

### 3. AI Recommendations

**How it works:**
1. AI analyzes your highest emission categories
2. Suggests targeted actions
3. Shows impact (CO₂ saved, $ saved, time needed)
4. Updates as your data changes

**Action Levels:**
- 🟢 **Easy:** Small habit changes (1-2 weeks)

## ❓ Common Questions

### "My footprint seems high, is that normal?"
Yes! Average global footprint is 4,700 kg/year. The goal is to get below 2,000 kg for sustainability.

### "How accurate are the calculations?"
They're estimates based on standard factors. For exact numbers, consider professional carbon audits.

### "Can I edit past entries?"
Currently not available. Focus on today and moving forward!

### "Where is my data stored?"
Locally in your browser (localStorage). No account needed, but no cloud sync.

### "Can I export my data?"
Coming soon! For now, take screenshots of your charts.
