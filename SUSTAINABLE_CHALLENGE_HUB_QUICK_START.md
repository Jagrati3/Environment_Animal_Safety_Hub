# 🌱 Sustainable Living Challenge Hub - Quick Start Guide

**Welcome to your sustainability journey!** This guide will help you get started in just 5 minutes.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open the Hub
Open `frontend/pages/sustainable-challenge-hub.html` in your web browser.

### Step 2: Explore Challenges
Click through the **Discover** tab to browse 30+ environmental challenges across 8 categories.

### Step 3: Join Your First Challenge
1. Find a challenge that interests you (we recommend starting with "Recycling Master" 🌱)
2. Click **"Join Challenge"**
3. Watch the confetti celebration! 🎉

### Step 4: Make Your First Check-in
1. Go to the **Active** tab
2. Click **"Check In Today"**
3. Add a note (optional)
4. Click **"Complete Check-in"**
5. Watch your progress grow!

## 🎮 How to Earn Points & Badges

### Points System
- **Join Challenges:** Get started!
- **Daily Check-ins:** Earn points for each check-in
- **Complete Challenges:** Big point bonuses!
- **Streaks:** Keep checking in daily for streak bonuses
- **Difficulty Multipliers:**
  - 🌱 Beginner: 1x points
  - 🌿 Intermediate: 1.5x points
  - 🌳 Expert: 2x points

### Badge Collection
Unlock 30 unique badges by:
- ✅ **Completing Challenges** (1, 5, 10, 25, 50)
- 🔥 **Building Streaks** (7, 30, 100 days)
- 🏆 **Earning Points** (1K, 5K, 10K)
- 📂 **Mastering Categories** (5 challenges per category)
- 🎯 **Special Achievements** (Perfect Week, Early Bird, etc.)

## 🆘 Common Questions

### Q: What happens if I miss a day?
**A:** Your challenge progress continues, but your streak resets. Don't worry—you can still complete the challenge!

### Q: Can I join multiple challenges?
**A:** Absolutely! Join as many as you can handle. We recommend 2-3 active challenges at a time.

### Q: How do I unlock badges?
**A:** Badges unlock automatically when you meet requirements. Check the Profile tab to see locked badges and their requirements.

### Q: What's the difference between difficulty levels?
**A:**
- **Beginner:** Easy lifestyle changes, minimal disruption
- **Intermediate:** Requires planning and commitment
- **Expert:** Significant lifestyle changes, maximum impact

### Q: Can I restart a failed challenge?
**A:** Yes! Simply join the challenge again from the Discover tab.

### Q: Where is my data stored?
**A:** All data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). No account needed, completely private!

### Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?
**A:** Currently, data is device-specific. Cloud sync is planned for Phase 2!

### Q: How accurate are the impact metrics?
**A:** Impact calculations are based on EPA data and environmental research. Actual impact may vary based on individual circumstances.
